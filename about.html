About SkyRoute

SkyRoute is a student project by Almukhan Abdrakhman and Tasbolat Kagan (SE-2502), built for the Web Technologies course at Astana IT University. It helps travellers compare domestic routes from Astana and practise the steps of booking a flight.

It is a demo: flights, prices and bookings are examples, and no real tickets are sold.

  • Home base: Astana (NQZ)
  • Nine nonstop domestic routes
  • Fares from $28 one-way
